# Bootstrap prompts

The main lab hands you a finished repo. That is a lie of convenience — it makes
the skills, scripts and templates look like things that arrive from somewhere
else.

They do not. Every one of them was generated by Pi in a session much like the
one you are running, then reviewed and kept.

These prompts let you generate them yourself and compare. Each ends by diffing
your version against the shipped one, because the comparison is the lesson:
you will find places where yours is better.

| Prompt | Generates | Time | Where it fits |
|---|---|---|---|
| `b1-agents-md.md` | `AGENTS.md` | 10 min | Before Part 3 |
| `b2-script.md` | `tools/run_sweep.py` | 15 min | Instead of Part 6's script |
| `b3-skill.md` | A new skill from your own session | 20 min | After Part 8. **The good one** |
| `b4-todo.md` | `TODO.template.md` | 5 min | Anywhere |

If you only do one, do **b3**. Writing a skill from a transcript of work you
just finished is the moment the whole idea clicks.
